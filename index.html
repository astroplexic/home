Intro


Jerry is a multidimensional energy intuitive who specializes in channeling Light Language Activations, Sound Healing, Shamanic Sound Journeys, and Oracle Deck Divination.

A clairvoyant who has the ability to see, sense, and interact with energy in the form of colors, sacred geometry, sound frequencies, and symbols. He uses his spiritual gifts to assist people in healing and transforming their lives by remembering their connection to source.

Jerry is based in the Los Angeles, California area where he offers his services in person and through online sessions.

Currently he is engaged in producing music, live looping, projection mapping, and fun experiments with TouchDesigner.

Background

2018 - Founded Photonic House, a multi-sensory immersive light art and soundscape museum experience in Salt Lake City, Utah, US.

2015 - Founded Tellomee, a peer-to-peer digital marketplace for sharing and discovering uniquely curated local experiences.

Check out his work here.

Jerry studied Cognitive Behavioural Science and Entrepreneurship at the University of Utah, where he was most fascinated with the study of consciousness, cognition, and emotions.

Since childhood, he has been an ET (Extraterrestrial) Experiencer and is intrigued by various personal moments with metaphysical and paranormal phenomena.
