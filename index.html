Intro


Jerry is a multidimensional energy intuitive who specializes in channeling Light Language Activations, Sound Healing, Shamanic Sound Journeys, and Oracle Deck Divination.

As a clairvoyant, he has the ability to see, sense, and interact with energy in the form of colors, sacred geometry, sound frequencies, and symbols. He uses his spiritual gifts to assist people in healing and transforming their lives by remembering their connection to source.

Based in the Los Angeles, California area, Jerry offers his services both in person and online. To learn more about how he can help you to reconnect to your higher consciousness and awaken your inner light, check out his services.

Currently he is engaged in producing music, live looping, projection mapping, fun experiments with TouchDesigner, and hanging out with nature.
